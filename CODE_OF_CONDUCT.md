# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

Examples of positive behavior:
- Be respectful and constructive in discussions.
- Assume good intent and give actionable feedback.
- Focus on ideas, not individuals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or ban accounts for violations.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to project maintainers through a private GitHub contact channel.
