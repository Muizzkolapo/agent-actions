# Account access

## Reset your password

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. We send a reset email that works for one
hour.

## Change your email address

Open Settings > Account, choose Email and enter the new address. We send a code to the
new address to confirm it.

## Suspicious messages

We never ask for your password. A message that offers you a cruise or a prize, or asks
you to click a link to claim it, is not from us. Delete it.
